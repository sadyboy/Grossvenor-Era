APP REVIEW NOTES — Grosvenor Era

A screen recording is attached showing the full app flow from launch.

1. The recording covers app launch, onboarding, all five game rooms, the profile screen, and the journal. There are no accounts, no login, and no account deletion — the app works entirely without sign-in. No paid features or in-app purchases exist.

2. Grosvenor Era is a single-player puzzle collection set in a Victorian Mayfair club. It has five games: a tile-placement strategy puzzle, a logic deduction mystery, a liquid-sort tea puzzle, a heraldry quiz, and a coat-of-arms design tool. It's aimed at adults who want thoughtful games with no ads or subscriptions.

3. No setup needed. The app opens to onboarding on first launch, then straight to the club hall. All five rooms are accessible immediately with no credentials required.

4. No external services are used. No APIs, analytics, authentication, or payment processors. All progress is saved locally via UserDefaults.

5. The app works the same in every region. No localisation differences.

6. All content is original — game mechanics, text, and illustrations were created by the developer. No licensed or third-party material is used.
